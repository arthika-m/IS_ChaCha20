UNDERSTAND THE CONCEPT

How does ChaCha20 work?

01

Initialization

Creates the internal state using constants, key, counter and nonce.

02

Quarter Round

Uses addition, XOR and bit rotation operations.

03

20 Rounds

Performs ten double rounds to mix the internal state.

04

XOR Operation

Combines plaintext with the generated keystream to produce ciphertext.

ChaCha20 Formula

Ciphertext = Plaintext XOR Keystream

Plaintext = Ciphertext XOR Keystream
